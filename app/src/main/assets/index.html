💳 Pay with M-Pesa

You enter your PIN only in Safaricom's own M-Pesa screen. This app never sees it. When the confirmation SMS arrives, the payment is filed under the category you chose here.

Open your Messages app, copy your M-Pesa SMS (one or many), and paste them here. Repeats are skipped automatically.

🔒 Security

If you forget your PIN, the only way back in is clearing the app's data, which deletes your records. Make a backup below first.

🔔 Alerts & fingerprint

Pop-up alerts open a small window with Sent / Paid buttons when an M-Pesa SMS arrives, instead of a notification on the bar. They need the "Display over other apps" permission. Fingerprint unlock needs a PIN set first, as its backup.
